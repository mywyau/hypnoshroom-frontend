# Hypnoshroom contractor job board

## Current focus

Help UK software contractors find an open role, understand the advertised terms and apply to the hiring team. Employer-submitted briefs supply the board. Public procurement intelligence is paused.

## Pilot

Start with UK Scala and backend teams that can supply a real brief and review applicants. Ask for the stack, rate or range, duration, remote or onsite expectations, advertised IR35 status and the application route. Ask the poster to close the listing when the role is filled or withdrawn.

Use the existing employer accounts, contract board and comparison tools. Test whether a contractor can find a relevant role, assess it and apply directly. Follow up with pilot employers about applications they reviewed, interviews and whether they would post their next opening. Treat click-throughs as interest only.

## Data

Describe advertised terms as observations from Hypnoshroom listings. Collect hiring outcomes through explicit employer feedback. Revisit aggregate reporting when the board has a recurring supply of briefs and enough observations to report meaningful sample sizes.
